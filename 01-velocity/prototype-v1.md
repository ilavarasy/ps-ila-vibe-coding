## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** It looks like the product tries to sell something. as it has a focused number in that page. Trying to solve the problems in the selling.
- **What confused them:** lack of context for me. I see a big number and i don't know what this is about. it looks like a dashboard for somebody. that person might know it but without context I did not understand.
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** it is highlighting something and it steels the focus from other things. so somone can focus on the large number and decide from it.
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** THey were not able to udnerstand what the insight was for. It was not clear to the reviewer that it was a dashboard providing the most important insight. Highlighting the large number alone did not give the context to the user of the dashboard on what it actually meant.
