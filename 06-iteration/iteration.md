## What the evidence says

_What real usage showed: numbers if your tool has analytics, counted behaviour if it does not. Put the signal that matters on screen._

- **Primary signal:** Bounce rate dropped from 60% to 33%, a 27-percentage-point improvement, suggesting the redesigned dashboard is encouraging users to explore further.
- **What moved:** Users are exploring beyond the first screen, with 15.75 views per visit and an average session duration of 9m 9s. This suggests encouraging early engagement.
- **What didn't:** We haven't validated whether users are taking the recommended action. The analytics don't show action clicks or whether users understood the insights. With only four visitors, it's too early to conclude that the hypothesis is validated.
